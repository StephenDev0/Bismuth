## References

No other third-party code is included. These parts were written from published descriptions:

- Band-limited audio synthesis follows the technique of blargg's blip_buf.
- Scale2x and Scale3x follow the AdvanceMAME ScaleNx algorithm by Andrea Mazzoleni.
- MD5 follows RFC 1321.
- The NES memory layout for RetroAchievements matches the region table in rcheevos (MIT License).
- Hardware behaviour, timing tables and the 2C02G signal levels come from the NESdev Wiki.
